Calorie Multiplier Calculator

This calculator uses body-weight calorie multipliers sourced from the NIDDK to help estimate daily caloric requirements.

How It Works & How to Use It

To use the calculator manually, choose your activity target from the sourced multiplier table below and multiply your body weight in pounds by the given range. Alternatively, type your weight into the interactive tool below for instant calculations.

Activity Level Calorie Multiplier Range Daily Calorie Target Purpose
Sedentary / Light Activity 11 – 13 calories / lb Fat Loss / Caloric Deficit
Moderate Activity 14 – 15 calories / lb Weight Maintenance
Heavy / Intense Activity 16 – 17 calories / lb Weight Gain / Caloric Surplus
