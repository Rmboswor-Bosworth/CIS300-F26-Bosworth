Nutrition Training & Habit Building

Building sustainable nutrition habits and understanding macronutrients are essential steps toward achieving long-term fitness goals.

Evidence-Based Research

To explore un-biased fitness research and evidence-based supplement analysis, visit Examine.com. It serves as a primary hub for learning how dietary choices impact exercise performance.

Meal Planning & Food Groups

Learn how balancing essential macronutrients fuels your gym performance. To review food group guidelines and start building healthy meal plans, explore USDA MyPlate.

Federal Recommendations & Caloric Metrics

For official dietary advice, refer to the Dietary Guidelines for Americans. Additionally, access the NIDDK Health & Nutrition Hub for clinical research regarding body weight management and physical activity.
